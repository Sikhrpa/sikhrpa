The Foundation of Safety

4 Universal Rules of Firearm Safety

Education is your first line of defense. Commit these rules to memory before handling any firearm.

■ Rule 1 of 4

Assume Every Firearm is Loaded

Treat every firearm as if it is loaded, even if you believe it is cleared or empty.

ਪੰਜਾਬੀ: ਹਮੇਸ਼ਾ ਇਹ ਮੰਨ ਕੇ ਚੱਲੋ ਕਿ ਹਥਿਆਰ ਲੋਡ (Loaded) ਹੈ

■ Rule 2 of 4

Never Point at Anything You Do Not Want to Destroy

Keep the muzzle pointed in a safe direction at all times during handling and transport.

ਪੰਜਾਬੀ: ਪਿਸਟਲ ਦਾ ਮੂੰਹ ਹਮੇਸ਼ਾ ਸੁਰੱਖਿਅਤ ਦਿਸ਼ਾ ਵੱਲ ਰੱਖੋ

■ Rule 3 of 4

Keep Your Finger Off the Trigger

Keep your finger outside the trigger guard until your sights are aligned on the target and you are ready to shoot.

ਪੰਜਾਬੀ: ਆਪਣੀ ਉਂਗਲ ਟਰਿੱਗਰ (Trigger) ਤੋਂ ਦੂਰ ਰੱਖੋ

■ Rule 4 of 4

Know Your Target & What Lies Beyond

Be completely certain of your target and what is located behind it before pulling the trigger.

ਪੰਜਾਬੀ: ਆਪਣੇ ਨਿਸ਼ਾਨੇ ਅਤੇ ਉਸਦੇ ਪਿੱਛੇ ਦੀ ਜਗ੍ਹਾ ਨੂੰ ਜਾਣੋ

Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential guidance on secure storage, legal responsibilities, and training.

What are the California safe storage requirements?

California law mandates that firearms be stored in a DOJ-approved locked container (safe or lockbox) or disabled with a federally approved locking device (trigger lock / cable lock) when left unattended, preventing unauthorized access by minors or prohibited persons.

How can first-time firearm owners join or participate in seminars?

SikhRPA organizes regular educational workshops and community seminars across California. You can reach out directly via email at president@sikhrpa.org for upcoming seminar schedules and registration details.

Is SikhRPA a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization?

Yes, the Sikh Rifle and Pistol Association is a California Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation organized exclusively for educational and charitable purposes under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code (Federal EIN: 42-2595402).
