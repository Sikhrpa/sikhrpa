The 4 Universal Tenets

Universal Firearm Safety Rules

Standard safety rules taught worldwide in English and Gurmukhi.

1

Always Treat Every Firearm as Loaded

Never accept someone's word that a firearm is unloaded. Personally check the chamber and magazine well every time you pick up or handle any firearm.

ਹਮੇਸ਼ਾ ਇਹ ਮੰਨ ਕੇ ਚੱਲੋ ਕਿ ਹਥਿਆਰ ਲੋਡ ਹੈ।

2

Never Point at Anything You Do Not Wish to Destroy

Maintain strict muzzle discipline at all times. Keep the barrel pointed downrange or in an indisputably safe direction.

ਬੈਰਲ ਦਾ ਮੂੰਹ ਹਮੇਸ਼ਾ ਸੁਰੱਖਿਅਤ ਦਿਸ਼ਾ ਵੱਲ ਰੱਖੋ।

3

Keep Your Finger Off the Trigger Until Ready to Shoot

Rest your index finger along the frame outside the trigger guard until your sights are on target and you have made the conscious decision to shoot.

ਟਰਿੱਗਰ 'ਤੇ ਉਂਗਲ ਉਦੋਂ ਹੀ ਰੱਖੋ ਜਦੋਂ ਨਿਸ਼ਾਨਾ ਪੂਰਾ ਹੋਵੇ।

4

Know Your Target and What Is Beyond It

Projectiles penetrate walls and barriers. Always confirm a solid, reliable backstop before initiating fire.

ਆਪਣੇ ਨਿਸ਼ਾਨੇ ਅਤੇ ਉਸਦੇ ਪਿੱਛੇ ਦੀ ਜਗ੍ਹਾ ਦਾ ਧਿਆਨ ਰੱਖੋ।

Frequently Asked Questions

What is SikhRPA's nonprofit tax status?

The Sikh Rifle and Pistol Association is an IRS-recognized 501(c)(3) nonprofit public benefit corporation organized in California. Contributions are tax-deductible to the extent permitted by law (EIN: 42-2595402).

Is the Firearm Safety Certificate (FSC) practice test free?

Yes, 100% free with no registration required. The interactive test provides detailed explanations referencing official California Penal Codes.

How do I request a clinic for my local community or Gurdwara?

Contact us at president@sikhrpa.org. We coordinate with local certified instructors and community centers to deliver safe handling and storage workshops.
