Receipt & Reimbursement Guidance

IRS § 170 Tax Substantiation

The IRS requires formal written acknowledgment for charitable contributions. This applies both to direct monetary gifts and to unreimbursed volunteer out-of-pocket expenses incurred on behalf of SikhRPA.

Out-of-Pocket: Deduct or Reimburse?

When you purchase supplies (e.g. safety locks, guidebooks, targets), you can choose: (1) Full Reimbursement repaid via Zelle/check, or (2) Donate the Expense to receive an official tax deduction letter.

Corporate Matching

Many employers match donations 1:1 or 2:1. Search for Sikh Rifle and Pistol Association or Federal EIN 42-2595402 in Benevity, CyberGrants, or your internal company giving portal.
