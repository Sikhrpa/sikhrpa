Giving Guidance

Charitable Contribution FAQs

Are contributions 100% tax-deductible?

Yes. The Sikh Rifle and Pistol Association is recognized by the Internal Revenue Service as a tax-exempt organization under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code (Federal EIN: 42-2595402). Contributions are tax-deductible to the fullest extent permitted by federal and California state law.

Can my company match my charitable gift?

Yes. Many employers match charitable donations made by their staff, doubling or tripling your impact. Contact your human resources or corporate social responsibility department and submit our EIN (42-2595402) through your company's matching gift portal.

How are donation funds audited and allocated?

As a California nonprofit public benefit corporation, all disbursements are strictly governed by our board of directors and allocated exclusively to our exempt educational mission: creating public safety literature, distributing safety cable locks, and hosting community safe-handling clinics.

We are a California Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation organized exclusively for educational and charitable purposes under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.
