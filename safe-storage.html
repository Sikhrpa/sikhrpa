    Criminal Storage & Child Access (PC § 25100)

    California law holds firearm owners strictly accountable if a minor under 18 or a prohibited person gains access to an unsecured firearm in the home (PC § 25100–25135) or when left unattended in a vehicle (PC § 25140). Always store firearms unloaded inside a locked safe or steel lockbox when not under your direct control.

    ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਬੱਚਿਆਂ ਜਾਂ ਅਣਅਧਿਕਾਰਤ ਵਿਅਕਤੀਆਂ ਦੀ ਪਹੁੰਚ ਤੋਂ ਦੂਰ, ਹਥਿਆਰ ਹਮੇਸ਼ਾ ਤਾਲਾ ਲੱਗੇ ਸੇਫ਼ ਜਾਂ ਲਾਕਬਾਕਸ ਵਿੱਚ ਰੱਖੋ।

    Verify the CA DOJ Safety Device Roster

    When buying a lockbox or safe to satisfy California's Firearms Safety Device (FSD) requirement, confirm your exact model SKU on the official CA DOJ roster. Many thin polycarbonate travel boxes are not CA DOJ certified—choose heavy 10-to-16-gauge steel lockboxes bolted down or tethered with a steel cable.

    Rust Control, Ammo Separation & Lead Hygiene

    Coastal fog and garage temperature swings quickly rust blued steel—drop a cordless silica dehumidifier inside your safe and keep a light coat of CLP on metal surfaces. Store bulk ammo in gasket-sealed, padlocked ammo cans, and always wipe hands with D-Wipe towels or wash with cold water before entering the house.

    California Transport & Safe Storage FAQs

    How do you legally transport a handgun in an SUV or hatchback in California?

    Because an SUV, hatchback, or minivan does not have a separate locked trunk, California Penal Code § 25610 and § 16850 require an unloaded handgun to be secured inside an independent, fully enclosed locked container such as a steel lockbox, padlocked hard case, or padlocked zippered range bag.

    Does a locking glovebox or center console count as a locked container in California?

    No. California Penal Code § 16850 explicitly excludes a motor vehicle's utility compartment and glove compartment from the definition of a locked container, even if it locks with a key.

    Can loaded magazines be in the same locked case as an unloaded gun in California?

    Under People v. Clark (1996), loaded magazines that are completely detached from an unloaded firearm may be transported in the same locked container. However, keeping ammunition in a separate sealed ammo can is best practice to eliminate confusion at ranges or during traffic stops.
