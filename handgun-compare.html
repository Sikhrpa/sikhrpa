How Size Governs Concealed Carry in California

Many first-time California CCW permit holders purchase a full-size or standard compact handgun (like a Glock 19) only to find it uncomfortable during daily seated routines or summertime attire.

  • • Grip Length Dictates Printing: The butt of the grip accounts for over 80% of accidental garment printing when bending over or twisting.
  • • Slide Length Aids Keel Principle: A slightly longer slide (e.g. 3.5" to 4.0") actually prevents the gun from tipping outward over your belt line in appendix carry (AIWB).
  • • Width (Thickness) Governs Comfort: Slim micro-compacts (1.0" wide) distribute pressure much more gently against the pelvic bone than double-stacks (1.3"+ wide).

Understanding the California Roster (PC § 32000)

In California, licensed gun dealers (FFLs) may only sell new handguns listed on the California Roster of Certified Handguns, unless transferred via private party (PPT) or legal intra-familial gift.

Recent 2024 Roster Expansions:

Recent legal challenges (*Boland v. Bonta*, *Renna v. Bonta*) prompted manufacturers to release specialized California models featuring manual safeties, loaded chamber indicators (LCI), and magazine disconnect mechanisms—introducing modern models like the Sig P365, Springfield Hellcat, and M&P Shield Plus to California counters.

How Assets are Scrubbed & Physically Calibrated

To achieve accurate 1:1 true physical scale comparison, raw studio photography and press assets are processed through our automated imaging pipeline (scripts/process_handgun_assets.py):

  1. Background Scrubbing: Neutral/white studio backdrops are converted to transparent alpha channels (RGBA).
  2. Tight Bounding Box Trim: All surplus empty margins are cropped tight to the physical outer extremities (muzzle to beavertail, sights to baseplate).
  3. 1:1 Dimensional Scaling: Assets are resized using high-order Lanczos resampling so that pixel width and height correspond strictly to lengthIn * PPI and heightIn * PPI.
  4. EXIF & Tracking Scrub: All camera metadata, geolocation, and tracking headers are stripped.
